AI Data Center Power Crunch

Grid bottleneck & clean energy plays. Compute demand is no longer gated by GPUs alone — interconnection queues and baseload scarcity now set the pace of AI capex.

01 Macro Trigger

Hyperscaler power PPAs outrun regional grid capacity; interconnection backlogs stretch 4–7 years.

02 Target Sector

Independent power, nuclear, natural gas midstream, and electrical equipment.

03 Key Tickers

VST · CEG · GEV · NEE · ETN

04 Tactical Risk

Policy delay, demand overstatement, and rate-sensitive multiple compression in utilities.

High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) & Semiconductor Capex Cycle

HBM is the binding constraint on AI accelerators. Memory pricing power and advanced-packaging capex are the second-order trade after the GPU headline.

01 Macro Trigger

HBM sold-out through 2026; GPU shipments wait on stacked-memory yield, not foundry wafers.

02 Target Sector

HBM producers, foundry, and WFE (deposition, etch, advanced packaging).

03 Key Tickers

MU · TSM · AMAT · LRCX · 000660.KS

04 Tactical Risk

Hyperscaler capex pause, export-control shocks, and a classic memory inventory cycle.

Small-cap Rotation vs. Mega-cap Tech Divergence

Index concentration is a feature until breadth turns. When the rate path and credit conditions ease, capital historically leaves a handful of megacaps for neglected cyclicals.

01 Macro Trigger

Soft-landing rate-cut path plus a widening equal-weight vs. cap-weight gap.

02 Target Sector

Russell 2000, regional banks, and industrial cyclicals versus mega-cap tech.

03 Key Tickers

IWM · KRE · XLI vs. QQQ · NVDA

04 Tactical Risk

Sticky inflation, a stronger dollar, and a liquidity fade that snaps capital back to quality.
