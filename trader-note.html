Operating System

Core Rules of the Trading Log

Why recording a 30-second voice note creates asymmetry. Written journals get sanitized. Voice captures hesitation, FOMO, and the actual trigger before P&L rewrites the memory. That raw tape lets you score process independent of outcome — and that is the only edge that compounds.

1. Record before the story

The memo is spoken within 30 seconds of the decision. After the fill, memory starts editing.

2. Voice first, text later

Emotion is data. Tone, pace, and certainty are part of the setup — they do not survive a tidy write-up.

3. Split process from P&L

A −1R with a clean stop is a pass. A green trade that broke a rule is a fail. Score the rule, not the dollar.

4. Never edit the original

Annotations are allowed. Rewriting the first take is how revenge trading hides inside a “review.”

5. One ticket, one memo

Adds, moves, and second entries need their own 30 seconds. Bundled stories hide the violation.

6. Weekly R and violation count

Asymmetry shows up in fewer repeated mistakes, not in a single winner. Count rule breaks before you count P&L.
